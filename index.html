You can watch premium IPTV on your iPhone or iPad in minutes. Use a reliable subscription like SubTV IP and a compatible player app.

This guide shows the exact steps for a smooth setup in 2026.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad (iOS 16 or later works best).
  • A premium IPTV subscription from SubTV IP — paid, instant activation, no free trial.
  • One of these player apps: IPTV Smarters Pro, IBO Player Pro, or Flex IPTV (all free on the App Store).
  • Optional: set Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1) to prevent ISP throttling — it helps with 4K / 60 fps HEVC streams.

Step-by-step setup

  1. Buy your subscription. Visit SubTV IP, choose 1, 3, or 12 months. Pay securely with PayPal or credit card. Activation is instant — you receive a playlist URL (M3U) and an Xtream Codes API link.
  2. Install a player. Open the App Store on your iPhone or iPad. Search for "IPTV Smarters Pro", "IBO Player Pro", or "Flex IPTV". Tap Get and install the app.
  3. Open the player and add your playlist. In IPTV Smarters Pro: tap "Login with Xtream Codes" or "Load Your Playlist or File/URL". Enter the URL or API details from your SubTV IP confirmation email.
  4. Set the buffer to 5,000 ms. Go to the player’s settings (usually a gear icon). Look for "Buffer Size" or "Cache". Change it to 5,000 ms. This reduces freezing during high-bitrate 4K streams.
  5. Configure DNS for anti-throttling. On your iPhone/iPad: go to "Settings > Wi-Fi > (i) next to your network > Configure DNS > Manual". Add 1.1.1.1 and remove the old DNS servers. This bypasses many ISP restrictions — a tip also recommended by Advanced Television for stable streaming.
  6. Start watching. Return to the player app. Your channels and VOD should load within seconds. Tap any stream to begin. The SubTV IP server cluster has 99.9% uptime, so you should see smooth playback even during peak hours.

If a step fails

Playlist doesn’t load? Double-check the URL or API link from your email. There are no typos. Contact SubTV IP support via WhatsApp for a corrected link.

Streams buffer or freeze? Make sure you set the buffer to 5,000 ms. Also confirm your Wi-Fi speed is above 25 Mbps for 4K content. Try switching to a 5 GHz network.

App crashes? Restart your iPhone or iPad. Update the player app via the App Store. If the issue persists, use a different player from the list (e.g., switch from IPTV Smarters Pro to IBO Player Pro).
